Questions

Answers before you have to ask.

The practical details venue and operations teams usually want to understand before a site review.

Who manages the machine? +

Heli manages installation, product supply, restocking, payment processing, cleaning routines, maintenance, repairs, customer support, and ongoing venue coordination.

What does the venue need to provide? +

The venue provides an approved indoor placement, a standard 120V outlet, visibility, customer access, service access, and required site approvals.

Is there an upfront cost? +

Qualified placements generally have no installation or equipment cost to the venue. Final commercial terms are confirmed during the site review.

How does the revenue share work? +

For applicable placements, the venue receives an agreed share of eligible machine sales. Heli provides transparent monthly reporting and payments according to the partnership agreement.

How much space is required? +

The machine measures approximately 38.7 inches wide by 36.6 inches deep and approximately 70 inches high. Additional space is needed for customer access, ventilation, and service clearance.

Is plumbing required? +

No. The machine is self-contained and requires standard electrical service rather than plumbing.

How is the unit kept clean and contained? +

Dispensing takes place inside an enclosed chamber designed to contain drips and spills. Heli performs scheduled cleaning, sanitation, refill, and maintenance visits and monitors the machine remotely.

Who restocks it? +

Heli monitors inventory and coordinates regular replenishment and service visits.

What happens if the machine needs service? +

Heli remotely monitors the unit and coordinates technical support and repairs. The venue team does not troubleshoot the equipment.

Can the machine be customized for our venue? +

Custom branding, customer promotions, launch offers, and venue-specific programs may be available depending on the partnership.

Can Heli operate outside normal business hours? +

The machine can generally operate whenever the surrounding space is accessible, subject to venue rules and the agreed operating schedule.

Is there a long-term commitment? +

The agreement depends on the placement. Initial pilot periods may be used so both Heli and the venue can confirm the fit before discussing a longer arrangement.
