Questions

Everything to know before you book.

Final details are always confirmed for the specific venue and event, but these are the questions we expect most often.

What types of events does Heli serve? +

Heli is available for weddings, birthdays, bridal and baby showers, corporate gatherings, resident events, wellness events, and brand activations.

Where do you provide event service? +

We serve Miami-Dade and select Broward locations, generally from South Miami to Fort Lauderdale. Availability depends on the venue, date, and event logistics.

What is included? +

Each event is quoted individually. The experience can include the styled Heli cart, Greek frozen yogurt, cups, spoons, napkins, a curated menu of toppings and sauces, event setup, and service coordination.

Can the cart be customized? +

Personalized signage, selected menu details, guest promotions, and brand-aligned event touches may be available depending on the event.

How is pricing determined? +

Standard event pricing is a $300 setup fee plus $9 per guest, with the service window determined by the selected package. Taxes, optional add-ons, travel outside Miami-Dade, and custom requirements may affect the final quote. See packages and pricing.

Can Heli be set up outdoors? +

Covered outdoor service may be possible depending on weather, shade, a flat surface, access, power, and venue requirements. We confirm the setup during planning.

How far in advance should I inquire? +

Availability varies by date and event logistics, so earlier inquiries are recommended. You can still submit a request for a near-term event and we will confirm whether the date is available.

Does the yogurt contain allergens? +

The frozen yogurt contains milk. Toppings and sauces may contain or be exposed to other common allergens. Ingredient and allergen details can be reviewed during menu planning.
