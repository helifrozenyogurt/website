The scoop

Good things
to know.

How much does a Heli event cost? +

Every event is quoted individually. Pricing depends on servings, service time, location, and setup needs. Send us the essentials and we’ll prepare a proposal for your event.

Does an inquiry reserve my date? +

No. The form starts a conversation about availability and your event quote. Your date and booking details are confirmed separately with Heli.

Can you serve my guest count? +

Tell us how many guests you expect and when you would like dessert served. We’ll review the serving quantity and service window before confirming the plan.

What types of events does Heli serve? +

We welcome inquiries for birthdays, showers, gender reveals, bar and bat mitzvahs, weddings, company gatherings, resident events, and brand activations. Every event is subject to availability and setup requirements.

Where do you provide event service? +

We serve Miami-Dade and select Broward locations, generally from South Miami to Fort Lauderdale. Availability depends on the venue, date, and event logistics.

What is included? +

Your proposal sets out the cart setup, frozen yogurt and topping menu, serving supplies, attendant service, and setup and breakdown, with the serving quantity and service window agreed for your event.

Can the cart be customized? +

Tell us what you have in mind. We’ll review what is possible for your event and include any agreed customization in the proposal.

Can Heli be set up outdoors? +

Outdoor service depends on weather, cover, a suitable surface, access, and compatible power. We review those requirements with you before confirming the setup.

Does the yogurt contain allergens? +

The frozen yogurt contains milk. Toppings and sauces may contain or be exposed to other common allergens. Ingredient and allergen details can be reviewed during menu planning.
