Pricing Questions

The helpful details before you book.

Simple pricing, transparent inclusions, and a final quote tailored to your date and venue.

How does Heli event pricing work? +

Events start at $480 for 20 confirmed guests and 60 minutes of service. Every Heli event includes a one-time $300 cart setup and service charge, plus $9 for each confirmed guest. Sixty minutes of service are included. Extend the experience to 90 minutes for $75 or to two hours for $150.

The one-time event setup includes:

  • Miami-Dade delivery
  • Heli cart and umbrella
  • Event setup
  • Heli attendant
  • Service coordination
  • Cleanup
  • Breakdown
What does “unlimited” mean? +

Unlimited Heli cups are available to your confirmed guest count during the booked service window while active event service is taking place. Service ends when the selected window concludes.

How does the final guest count affect pricing? +

The event-size cards show starting totals. Your final quote adjusts to your confirmed guest count and selected service window, along with any travel or optional event details.

What if I have more than 75 guests? +

We would be happy to prepare a custom service plan for a larger event. Send us the guest count, date, and venue area so we can recommend the right timing and setup.

Is a deposit required? +

Your final quote will include the booking and payment details for your specific event. Submitting an availability request does not create a booking or commitment.
