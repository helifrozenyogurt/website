Pricing Questions

The helpful details before you book.

Simple pricing, transparent inclusions, and a final quote tailored to your date and venue.

What does “unlimited” mean? +

Every guest included in your booked guest count may enjoy unlimited Heli cups during your selected service window. The offer ends when the scheduled service time concludes.

Why is the setup fee separate? +

The fixed setup fee covers the logistics that are required for every event, including the cart, Miami-Dade delivery, setup, staffing, service coordination, cleanup, and breakdown. The per-guest price covers the frozen yogurt, toppings, and serving supplies.

Can I extend the service time? +

Yes, additional service time is $75 per 30 minutes and is subject to availability and venue logistics.

What if I have more than 75 guests? +

We would be happy to prepare a custom service plan for a larger event. Send us the guest count, date, and venue area so we can recommend the right timing and setup.

Is a deposit required? +

Your final quote will include the booking and payment details for your specific event. Submitting an availability request does not create a booking or commitment.
