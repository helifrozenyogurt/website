Questions

Answers before you have to ask.

The questions we hear most from property teams, answered up front.

Who manages the machine? +

Heli does, entirely. We handle installation, product supply, restocking, cleaning, maintenance, repairs, customer support, and refunds. Your team doesn't operate or troubleshoot anything.

What does the property need to provide? +

Just a standard 120V outlet and a small footprint of floor space — no plumbing, no buildout, and no staff time.

How much space does it take? +

The machine is 38.7" wide and 36.6" deep — about the footprint of a large refrigerator, plus clearance for service access.

Is there an upfront cost to the property? +

No. Heli covers installation and equipment. Most placements run on a revenue-share or simple partnership structure — we'll walk you through the right fit for your property during the site review.

How is it restocked? +

Heli monitors the machine remotely and schedules refills on a regular service visit cadence, so it stays stocked without any action from your team.

What happens if there's an issue? +

The machine has remote, real-time fault monitoring and shuts down automatically if something's wrong, so it never serves a bad product. Our team is notified immediately and dispatches service directly — you don't have to call anyone.

Is it clean and safe? +

Yes. The unit is fully self-contained, uses a sealed internal dispensing chamber, and follows a set sanitation schedule. Heli is fully insured and PCI-compliant for payments.

What kind of product is served? +

A 5 oz portion of premium Greek frozen yogurt with no sugar added and fat-free. It contains milk; full ingredient and allergen information is available on request.

Can we brand it or run promotions for our community? +

Yes. We offer custom machine branding and can set up resident, guest, staff, or student promo codes for launches and special days — we'll plan these with you during the site review.

Is it available 24/7? +

In most locations, yes — the machine runs on its own schedule, so guests or residents can use it any time the space is accessible, not just during staffed hours.

Is there a contract, and can we change our minds? +

We keep initial placements low-commitment — typically a pilot period — so both sides can confirm it's a good fit before anything longer-term is discussed.
